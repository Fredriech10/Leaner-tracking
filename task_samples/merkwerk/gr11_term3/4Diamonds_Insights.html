DIAMONDS INSIGHTS

Diamonds Insights is a South African initiative that helps young people understand how diamonds move from deep below the earth to the global jewellery market. The organisation promotes responsible sourcing and explains how the Four Cs influence the value of a polished diamond.

The Four Cs

  1. Cut
  2. Colour
  3. Clarity
  4. Carat

Learn more about professional diamond education at https://www.gia.edu/diamond-education
