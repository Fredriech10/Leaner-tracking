Digital Marketing

Digital marketing is the use of online channels (websites, email, search engines, and social media) to promote products, services, or ideas. It helps businesses reach the right audience, measure results, and improve campaigns over time.

What digital marketing includes

  • Website marketing
  • Social media marketing
  • Email marketing
  • Online advertising

Why it matters

  1. It reaches people where they spend time: online.
  2. It can be targeted (age, location, interests, search intent).
  3. It is measurable (clicks, sign-ups, purchases).
  4. It can be cost-effective compared to traditional media.

Key terms (quick glossary)

Term Meaning
SEO Search Engine Optimisation: improving your site to appear higher in search results.
CTR Click-Through Rate: percentage of people who clicked after seeing an ad or link.
Target audience The specific group you want to reach (e.g., parents, teens, small businesses).
