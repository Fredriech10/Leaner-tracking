Campaign Planning & Channels

Step-by-step campaign plan

  1. Set a goal (e.g., 100 sign-ups this month).
  2. Know the audience (who they are, what they need, where they spend time online).
  3. Choose channels (SEO, social media, email, paid ads).
  4. Create content (posts, a landing page, an offer, a video, etc.).
  5. Launch and measure (track clicks, sign-ups, sales).
  6. Improve (change what is not working; repeat what works).
