Global Tourism: An Overview

Global tourism refers to people travelling to destinations outside their usual environment for leisure, business, education, religion, sport, or other purposes. Tourism connects countries through culture, transport, trade, and shared experiences.

Why People Travel

  • Leisure and holidays (beaches, cities, nature)
  • Visiting friends and family
  • Business travel and conferences
  • Education and study tours
  • Religious tourism and pilgrimages
  • Adventure and sport tourism

Benefits of Tourism

Tourism can create jobs, support local businesses, and generate income for communities. It can also encourage cultural exchange and funding for conservation.

Challenges

Tourism can place pressure on local resources such as water, energy, and waste systems. Overcrowding, environmental damage, and loss of local culture can occur if tourism is not managed responsibly.
