1. Task and learner files

The starter document is copied for every learner. The question paper and support files are available as downloads in the task.

2. Marking criteria

Choose a Word action, then complete only the settings used by that action. The selection list is grouped by the Word ribbon area and generates a fixed, repeatable marking check.

Marking result label: the sentence shown with the learner's mark, for example: “Cover page author and abstract completed”. It does not determine what is marked. Expected state: Yes means the selected action must be present; No means it must be absent.

Table numbers, row numbers and column numbers start at 1 in the builder. The marker converts them to the internal document position automatically.
Total marks are calculated from the criteria.
