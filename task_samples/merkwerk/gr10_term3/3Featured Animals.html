Unique Animals of the World

Discover remarkable animals, where they live and what makes them unique.

Introduction

The Earth is home to an extraordinary variety of animals. Some species can regenerate body parts, detect electrical signals, survive in extreme climates or blend perfectly into their surroundings. Learning about these animals helps us understand biodiversity and the importance of conservation.

Featured Animals

Platypus

Where it is found: Australia and Tasmania.

The platypus is an egg-laying mammal with a duck-like bill, webbed feet and a broad tail. It detects tiny electrical signals produced by prey while hunting underwater.

Axolotl

Where it is found: Mexico.

The axolotl can regenerate limbs and parts of its spinal cord, heart and brain. Wild axolotls are critically endangered.

Protecting habitats, reducing pollution and preventing illegal wildlife trade can help unique animals survive. Every species contributes to a healthy ecosystem.
